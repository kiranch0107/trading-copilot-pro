  back. Converting them needs no predictive power: a trailing
  stop, a partial, or a break-even move acts on what already
  happened.

  RULE across the whole population. Multiplying this count by a
  hypothetical payoff is backtesting on the answer sheet.

  DEAD WRONG is 47% of losses — the ENTRY is the
  problem, which matches everything already measured: the setup
  readings carry no information at n = 1,457. Exit management
  cannot fix a trade that never moved our way.
